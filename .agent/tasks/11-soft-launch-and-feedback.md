# Task 11 — Soft launch and feedback

Status: Not started

Depends on: Task 10

## Objective

Validate the real experience with a small audience and turn observed problems into a stable first milestone.

## Actions

- Configure privacy-conscious basic analytics and document exactly what is collected.
- Recruit 5–10 testers and give them concrete tasks: find a beach, open a story, use filters, switch map/list, find tips, and share the direct story URL.
- Test across phones, tablets, desktop, keyboard, and assistive technology where possible.
- Record evidence, prioritize issues, fix release blockers, and retest.
- Prepare a small social package linking directly to the Sandy Beach story.
- Write a short release note and retrospective; tag the stable milestone.

## Acceptance criteria

- Tester findings are recorded as observed behavior, not general impressions only.
- High-severity usability/accessibility defects are fixed and retested.
- Analytics/privacy copy matches actual collection.
- The stable public milestone and direct Sandy Beach link are verified.
